Rate Decision Rules

A checklist before moving money or refinancing.

Deposit rateCompare after-tax value

Check minimum balances, withdrawal restrictions, insurance coverage, promotional expiration and whether the yield is APY rather than APR.

CD vs TreasuryMatch the time horizon

Compare maturity, liquidity, early-withdrawal penalties, state-tax treatment and reinvestment risk rather than rate alone.

RefinanceFees can erase savings

Compare total interest and break-even months. A lower monthly payment can still cost more if the term is extended significantly.

Credit cardAPR compounds fast

Prioritize high APR debt. For balance transfers, include transfer fees and the post-promo rate before moving a balance.

Built-in Contract Coverage

The watchlist is designed to cover inflation, consumer costs, industrial demand, interest rates and broad risk sentiment.

EnergyConsumer inflation

WTI (CL), Brent (BZ), Natural Gas (NG), RBOB Gasoline (RB) and Heating Oil / ULSD (HO). These can flow into fuel, utility, freight and goods costs.

MetalsInflation + growth

Gold (GC), Silver (SI), Copper (HG) and Platinum (PL). Copper is closely watched for industrial demand while precious metals often react to real rates, the dollar and risk demand.

Equity index futuresRisk sentiment

S&P 500 E-mini (ES), Nasdaq-100 E-mini (NQ), Dow E-mini (YM) and Russell 2000 E-mini (RTY) show broad U.S. equity risk appetite before and during the cash session.

Rates, dollar & agricultureMacro transmission

2Y / 10Y / 30Y Treasury futures (ZT / ZN / ZB), Dollar Index (DX), Corn (ZC), Wheat (ZW) and Soybeans (ZS) add policy-rate, currency and food-price context.

Futures prices can be volatile and contract specifications differ. This page is a tracker and interpretation tool, not trading advice or a live exchange feed.

.ICS Import

Compatible with exported calendars from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook and many market-calendar tools.

The importer reads VEVENT entries, preserves title/date/time/location/description where available, and automatically classifies common economic, earnings, conference-call, split and dividend keywords. Duplicate UIDs are skipped.
Calendar data is stored locally in this browser. Event classification is automatic but editable; always verify dates/times against the original source for trading-critical events.

CSV / EXCEL IMPORT

Import dashboard data or futures files

Choose CSV, XLSX or XLS. The importer scans every Excel sheet and recognizes common financial column names. Existing values are preserved unless a matching field or symbol is found.

Futures: use Symbol/Ticker/Contract + Last/Price/Close, Previous/Prev Close, Date, Open, High, Low, Volume. Yahoo-style symbols such as CL=F, NG=F and SI=F are recognized. A single-contract OHLC file can omit Symbol if its filename or Excel sheet is named with the contract.
Economy: indicator/series, latest/value, previous, date. Rates: category/name and rate/value. Retirement: field and value.
